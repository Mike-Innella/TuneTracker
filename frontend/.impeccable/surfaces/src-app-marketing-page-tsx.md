Scope: public homepage `/` (Persuade). App routes (dashboard, board, community, profile, settings) are Operate surfaces in the same world.
Audience/job: music creatives deciding whether to move their song projects here. Action: Get Started (sign up). Secondary: Learn More (scroll to how it works).
Proof on hand: the real product only. No testimonials, users or stats exist; demo content is synthetic and labelled.
Constraints: pinned palette (midnight navy, electric blue, cyan, indigo, controlled violet; neon logo); dark default + designed light theme; WCAG 2.2 AA; reduced motion.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every project reads like the credits page of the record it will become. Refuses the category default of centred headline + glowing laptop screenshot + three icon feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Midnight-navy booklet stock (#060a1c → #0d1430) as the ground; the logo's cyan→blue→violet gradient is the single light source, used for glow behind the mark, the moving edge of drags, and primary actions only. Credits typography: Archivo condensed caps for roles/labels, Archivo expanded heavy italic for titles (echoing the poster wordmark), tabular JetBrains Mono for every number (track no., BPM, key, durations, dates). Hairline credit rules, dotted leaders between role and name. Light theme = day sleeve: cool white stock, navy ink, same gradient.

STORY: A visitor sees that a song's whole journey (ideas → mix → master → release) fits a board shaped like their workflow, that collaborators are credited with real roles, and that sharing a demo is one explicit step. They sign up.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 5/12: kicker "Side A — Create · Collaborate · Share", title "TuneTracker 2.0" in expanded heavy italic, one-line offer, Get Started (gradient, primary) + Learn More (ghost). Right 7/12: a live demo board titled like a tracklist ("Midnight Drive — Production"), 3 columns, draggable synthetic tasks with BPM/key in mono, labelled "Demo board — try dragging". Neon logo glow blooms behind the board's top-left. Credits strip below: "Produced by you · Mixed with collaborators · Shared with the community".

FORM: Liner Notes — candidate 7 of 7 grounded (album credits booklets / tracklist sleeves); seed:039a85b6bd15998b. Raises: claim→proof pairing (Claim & Proof), designed empty states (Seven-Segment), only the moving edge takes colour (Sliding Planes), one numeric voice (Glyph Render). Signature interaction: drag on the hero demo board, the card and its drop slot light in the gradient, everything at rest stays navy. Motion grammar: one staggered "needle drop" reveal of tracklist rows on entry; no scattered hover effects.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
